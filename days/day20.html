Scavi di Pompei
📍 Two entrances — Porta Marina 40.7485, 14.4831 · Piazza Anfiteatro (near Piazza Immacolata) 40.7477, 14.4876  ·  📞 +39 081 536 9869
Anfiteatro romano, Pompei
  • Hours: Daily 8:30am-7pm, last entry 5:30pm.
  • Gap closed — 2026 visitor caps: the Parco Archeologico now enforces a daily visitor limit (20,000/day) with time-slot caps during the main season (1 Apr-31 Oct): 9am-1pm capped at 15,000 entries, 1pm-5:30pm capped at 5,000. Late September falls inside this window — book a timed-entry slot online in advance rather than treating this as optional, especially given the revised, later arrival time.
  • Two entrances exist — reviewers note the choice of entrance affects which direction you naturally walk the site (Porta Marina, furthest from central Naples, lets you start at the amphitheatre end and work back). Worth deciding this in advance rather than defaulting to whichever entrance the GPS suggests.
  • Luggage: use the official Deposito Bagagli rather than leaving anything in the car — a real safety point, not just itinerary padding.
  • Time required: 2 hours minimum, 3-4 if you want to see most of it. Given the revised, later realistic arrival, budget for the tighter end of that range.

Cool, Interesting & Quirky — a note for tomorrow morning

Gabinetto Segreto, Museo Archeologico Nazionale di Napoli (MANN)
📍 40.8536, 14.2512 — Piazza Museo Nazionale 19
The Alexander Mosaic, from Pompeii's House of the Faun (now in MANN)

If there's a genuine free hour before heading to Sansevero tomorrow, MANN's "Secret Cabinet" is worth flagging: a room of erotic frescoes, mosaics, and objects recovered directly from the Pompeii and Herculaneum excavations — kept locked away from general view for roughly two centuries after being judged too explicit for public display, only consistently open to ordinary visitors since the 2000s. A genuine, if unusual, extension of today's Pompeii theme rather than an unrelated add-on. MANN's collection also holds the original Alexander Mosaic, discovered in Pompeii's House of the Faun — a direct thread from today's ruins to tomorrow's museum, if there's time for it.

Honest flag: MANN is closed Tuesdays (except when Tuesday is a public holiday, in which case closure shifts to Wednesday) — 29 September 2026 is a Tuesday, so this evening doesn't work for it; treat it as a Day 21 morning-only possibility if the Sansevero schedule leaves room, not tonight's plan. The Gabinetto Segreto specifically is also closed during MANN's free-entry openings for security reasons.
